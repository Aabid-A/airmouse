# offline - Power-Button Footer

Full prompt: [../prompts/offline-footer.md](../prompts/offline-footer.md)

A full-bleed footer scene: a beige vintage computer on a grassy hill under a blue
sky. The "o" in the wordmark "offline" is a power button. Clicking it plays the
clip forward: the sky turns to night and the screen collapses to a point of light.

## Use this when
- The footer should carry the brand's story or end on a moment.
- You can produce one short, locked-off video clip of the scene.

## Don't use when
- The footer must be compact or purely functional.

## Parts worth reusing
- **Wordmark letter as a control** (the "o" is an inline SVG power symbol matched to the serif's weight).
- **All animation comes from one clip** played forward on click; nothing drawn in code.
- **Responsive video**: 1080p, with a 720p file for screens 900px and narrower, plus a poster frame.
- **Top tint** (dark wash over the top 38%) to keep white text readable on a bright scene.

## Tokens
- Fonts: Instrument Serif 400 (wordmark and headings); Inter 400/500 (links).
- Colours: white text over the scene.
- Unit: 1u = 1/1902 of the viewport width.
