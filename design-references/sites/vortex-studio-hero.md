# Viktor Oddy - Studio Landing

Full prompt: [../prompts/vortex-studio-hero.md](../prompts/vortex-studio-hero.md)

White, narrow-column landing for a one-person design studio. Serif logo, a
two-line heading with serif accent words, three short paragraphs, two buttons, a
full-width infinite marquee of GIFs, then a big testimonial quote.

## Use this when
- Selling a person or small team, where trust and clarity matter more than spectacle.
- You want something light to build and fast to load.

## Don't use when
- The brand needs drama or a dark, immersive feel.

## Parts worth reusing
- **Narrow centred column** (`max-w-[440px]`) for the opening copy.
- **Serif accent words** inside a sans heading ("Build the *next wave*, the *bold way*").
- **Primary button with layered shadow and inset highlight**; plain secondary beside it.
- **Infinite marquee** of work previews (duplicated list, translate to -50%).
- **Staggered fade-in** with 0.1 s steps.

## Tokens
- Fonts: PP Neue Montreal (body), PP Mondwest (serif accent); mono for the tagline.
- Colours: white page, ink `#051A24`, heading `#0D212C`, author `#273C46`, pale `#F6FCFF`, `#E0EBF0`.
- Type: 32/40/44px heading, leading 1.1, tight tracking.
