# Mossline - Floating Moss Island Scroll Fly-In

Full prompt: [../prompts/moss-island.md](../prompts/moss-island.md)

A pinned scroll story for an environmental-data company. A floating moss island
under the headline "Measure the Living Planet"; scrolling scrubs a video that flies
into the island and lands in a moss landscape. Text writes in word by word, lime
hotspots pop up, a glass data card counts up, then a white solutions panel slides
up with tilting cards and stats.

## Use this when
- You want to tell a story in beats: hero, vision, proof, product, numbers.
- You have (or can generate) one cinematic fly-through video.

## Don't use when
- Visitors need information fast; pinned scroll stories slow them down.

## Parts worth reusing
- **Scroll-scrubbed video** with text beats timed to scroll position.
- **Foreground overlap**: ferns in front of the bottom of the headline letters.
- **Word-by-word text reveal** and **count-up numbers** with a drawing sparkline.
- **Lime chip tag**: 22px tall, mono 10.5px, uppercase, tracked .16em.
- **Glass data card** over the scene.
- **White panel that slides up** to end the story and start normal content.

## Tokens
- Fonts: Inter Tight 400/500/600; JetBrains Mono 400/500 for labels.
- Colours: ink `#10291a`, ink2 `#2c4434`, mist `#e7ebe8`, paper `#f6f7f4`, lime `#c6f35c`, lime2 `#a9dd3a`.
- Glass: `rgba(244,247,243,.16)` with white lines at 28%.
