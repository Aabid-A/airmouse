# Offcut - Streetwear Clothing-Rail Hero

Full prompt: [../prompts/clothing-rail.md](../prompts/clothing-rail.md)

Dark streetwear hero: a blackletter logo, a polished chrome rail across the page
and ten garments hanging edge-on in real CSS 3D that swing round to face you on
hover. A red marquee band runs along the bottom. Clicking a garment lifts it into
a centred detail view with a counter, italic serif name, price and arrows.

## Use this when
- A small product range with strong visuals and a bold brand voice.
- You want a hero that people play with.

## Don't use when
- The brand is soft, corporate or text-heavy.
- More than about a dozen products need to show at once.

## Parts worth reusing
- **CSS 3D hover-to-face** cards (no WebGL needed).
- **Chrome rail** built from a five-stop metallic gradient.
- **Detail view lift** with the rest of the page washed out.
- **Marquee band** as a loud brand stripe.
- **Type contrast**: blackletter logo, italic serif product names, plain Inter UI.

## Tokens
- Fonts: UnifrakturMaguntia (logo), Instrument Serif italic (names), Inter 400/500/600.
- Colours: bg `#0b0b0c`, ink `#f1efec`, mute `#8d8a86`, red `#b3121b`.
- Rail gradient: `#fafafa`, `#b9bcc1`, `#5d6066`, `#a9adb3`, `#e6e7e9`.
